# A.Grapes
## 题目详情
There are $N$ people numbered from $1$ to $N$.
There are $M$ grapes. They will be distributed to the $N$ people as follows.
-   Give one grape each to persons $1,2,\dots,N$ in this order. If the grapes run out midway, the distribution ends at that point.
-   If there are still grapes remaining after giving to person $N$, go back to person $1$ and distribute in order again. Repeat this until the grapes run out.
Find how many grapes each person receives.
## 提交详情
- 提交时间:20:02:41 状态:AC 详细状态:`-`
> grapes葡萄、distributed 分布  
把 $m$ 个葡萄均匀分到 $n$ 个人，那么每个人都会至少获得 $\frac{m}{n}$ 个葡萄  
然后剩余的 $m\bmod n$ 再从第一个人往后每人分一个
